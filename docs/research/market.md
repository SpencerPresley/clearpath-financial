# Market research: incumbent marketing compliance tools

Researched 2026-10. Labels: **[V]** vendor marketing claim, **[3P]** third party, **[P]** practitioner. Most details come from vendor pages; this is not verified product behavior.

## Landscape

The market splits three ways:
- **Broker-dealer / RIA tools** (FINRA/SEC): Red Oak, Hadrius, StarCompliance, ACA ComplianceAlpha. Saifr started here and later added a banking product.
- **Bank and lender tools:** PerformLine, Luthor, Sedric, Kadince, Social Assurance, Predict360, Persado, Fintel Check.
- **Generic proofing tools that banks use:** Ziflow, Workfront, Lytho.

**PerformLine** is the closest match for ClearPath. It serves banks, card issuers, fintechs, and mortgage lenders. [V]:
- Scores each asset 0–100, mapped to Pass/Warn/Fail, with the regulation cited and a suggested rewrite.
- Discovers brand pages on partner, lead-gen, and marketplace sites.
- Monitors calls and SMS.

Sources: [pre-pub](https://performline.com/products/documents/), [fintech](https://performline.com/industry/fintech/).

**Fintel Check** monitors partner URLs that the customer chooses. It runs rule checks on fees and APRs and reports changes. [V] [link](https://www.fintelconnect.com/products/fintel-check)

**AI pre-review with citations is now standard.** PerformLine, Luthor, Saifr, Persado, and Sedric all claim it. [V]

## Gaps

- **Approval expiry tied to offer terms:** none of the roughly 14 vendors surveyed documents it. That's absence from their marketing pages, not proof nobody does it.
- **No bank-published review SLA** was found. Coverage of a Persado-commissioned Cornerstone study reports "four to six weeks" at some institutions. That figure is vendor-commissioned and comes from secondary coverage only ([MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/marketing-tech/only-9-of-bank-marketing-teams-use-ai-agents-that-can-act.md)).
- **Approvals aren't linked to where content actually runs.** At a bank compliance roundtable [P], an attendee said: "We're reviewing posts on social media, not in our system. That's how we know it wasn't approved." ([PerformLine roundtable](https://performline.com/blog-post/bank-compliance-insights-managing-marketing-social-third-party-oversight/))

## How lenders oversee affiliates in practice [V]/[P]

- Compliance requirements go in the affiliate contract.
- Affiliates get an onboarding kit with pre-approved copy and disclosure templates.
- Monitoring frequency is set per partner by risk, with dated screenshots kept as evidence.
- Escalation: notice, then warning, then commission hold or suspension, then termination.

Sources: [Fintel](https://www.fintelconnect.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-compliance/), [The Search Monitor](https://www.thesearchmonitor.com/guide/affiliate-compliance-guide/).

Enforcement precedent: Amerisave (2014) advertised rates on a third-party site that it wouldn't honor ([Venable](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2014/08/cfpb-targets-mortgage-online-advertising-lead-gene)).
