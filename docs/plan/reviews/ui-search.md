# UI review: quick-jump and comment deep link

Branch `feat/comment-link-search` (topbar quick-jump ComboBox, `?comment=` deep link on the review screen). Spec
§8.4 short review. A fresh reviewer read spec §5 (5.1–5.7, all of it), the make-interfaces-feel-better skill
(SKILL.md and its checklist; animations.md and performance.md for the two motion pieces; surfaces.md and
typography.md by section), and ui-notifications.md for format and evidence bar. Code read:
`features/search/QuickJump.tsx`, `CommentsTab` in `features/review/tabs.tsx`, `reviewSearch.ts`, the
`ReviewPage.tsx` and `routes/items.$itemId.tsx` wiring, `ui/layout/focusMain.ts`, `ui/useHotkeys.ts`, the
`.app-search` rules (theme.css 1431–1441) and the `.app-jump*` / `.app-comment[data-linked]` block (3849–3903),
plus RAC 1.22.1 `private/ComboBox.mjs` for popover anchoring. Then I viewed every screenshot.

Screenshots in `docs/plan/reviews/ui/search/`. The PR's eleven at 1440×900: idle, focused, hint, results,
results-highlighted, legacy, no-match, searching, error, comment-linked, comment-linked-after. The reviewer's
captures, from throwaway `e2e/zz-review-*.spec.ts` (deleted) on the same stubs: `review-stale-rows-1440` (stale
rows at the moment of Enter), `review-slow-stale-1440` (stale rows while the next request is in flight),
`review-comment-mouse-open-1440` (comment opened by clicking the bell row), `review-comment-blurred-1440` (after
Tab leaves the linked comment), `review-left-with-text-1440`, `review-results-1280` (1280×800). Keyboard traces,
geometry, computed styles, the ARIA tree and RAC's live-announcer text are in `review-measurements.json` and
`review-escape.json`, with the retry, hover and Tab-count checks in `review-extra.json`. Keyboard observations were driven with real key presses and read back from
`document.activeElement`.

Verdict: **1 blocker, 4 should-fix, 3 nits.** The happy path works: ArrowDown+Enter and a mouse click both open the
right item and land focus on `#main`, the field's focus ring is clear on the navy bar, results carry status as
badge + glyph + label, and opening a mention from the bell focuses the comment. The blocker is that Enter acts on
rows from a previous query: it opens the wrong item, or does nothing, depending on timing. The should-fixes are what
screen readers hear, where Escape leaves you, what Enter will open, and the deep link re-firing every time you
come back to Comments.

## Keep

- **The deep link lands right from the bell.** Clicking the mention row: URL `…?tab=comments&v=2&comment=311`, focus on
  `li#comment-311`, comment in view (`mouseOpen`). By mouse, Chrome doesn't match `:focus-visible`, so you get the bar
  and tint without a ring (`review-comment-mouse-open-1440.png`); by keyboard you get the ring. `focusMain()` leaves
  focus alone when the page has already placed it, which is what makes that work. Reduced motion drops the animation
  (theme.css 3898–3902), and `aria-current="true"` marks the comment for assistive tech.
- **The happy paths are correct** (measured). ArrowDown ×2 + Enter → `/items/1061`; click on CP-1070 →
  `/items/1070`; both clear the box and focus `#main`. ⌘K works from inside a textarea; `/` is ignored in fields and
  modals (`useHotkeys`). `aria-keyshortcuts="Meta+K Control+K /"` is on the input.
- **Options read well.** Accessible names are "CP-1188 Fall personal loan promo — radio read (2025) MCR-26-045
  Approved": key, full title, tracker ID and status label. Status is a badge with glyph + label in its §5.2 tone
  (in review blue, approved green, changes requested amber), never color alone. Keys are mono with `tabular-nums`.
- **Field focus is visible on the navy bar** (`focused-1440.png`): 2px `focus-on-brand` outline on the whole field,
  and the ⌘K hint hides while focused, so the box doesn't carry a shortcut you're already using.
- **Copy is short and domain-native** (§5.4): "Jump to item (CP-1042 or title)", "Type a CP key, title words or
  tracker ID.", "No items match “zzz”." No sparkle, emoji or chatty copy (§5.6). The popover reuses `.app-popover`
  (opacity-only 100ms transition, one overlay shadow, `rounded-lg`, `p-1`, `rounded-md` rows).

## Fixture and method notes (not findings)

- The error state recovers by editing: with a stub that fails only the first request, "fall" → error → Backspace →
  "l" re-requested (`asked: ["fall","fal","fall"]`) and showed four results (`review-extra.json` `retype`). The
  `srErrorRetype` entry in `review-measurements.json` used an always-failing stub, so it doesn't show this. The copy
  is the server's message alone, as ruled in ui-notifications N7.
- Skill checklist items with nothing to report: the linked tint is a keyframe, which animations.md reserves for
  one-shot sequences, and this is one (S3 is about it replaying, not about the choice). No `transition: all`, no
  `will-change`.
- The "Ctrl K" hint in every shot is the headless Chromium UA; `isMac()` shows ⌘K on macOS.
- The queue's empty stat skeletons in every search shot come from `stubApi` lacking the counts endpoint, not from
  this PR.
- `review-escape.json` ran on the review page with a comment draft; `review-measurements.json` holds everything else.
  The ⌘K-inside-a-modal check didn't run: clicking Reject in that fixture opened no dialog.
- The concentric-radius and 40px hit-area rules are waived as in prior reviews (6px ceiling; the 32px topbar field
  and 36px result rows, as in ui-notifications.md).
