# UI review: Assistant panel and Outbox

Workstream K, branch `wsK/assistant`. Spec §8.4 review. A fresh reviewer read spec §5 (all of it),
reports/design-system.md, decisions.md "Assistant", and the make-interfaces-feel-better checklist, then viewed every
screenshot. Screenshots: `docs/plan/reviews/ui/assistant/<state>-<width>.png`, 25 states at 1440×900 and 1280×800,
plus two keyboard focus captures. Captured by `frontend/e2e/screenshots.assistant.spec.ts` (`SCREENSHOTS=1`) against
the production build, with `stubAssistant.ts` underneath and a per-shot scene layered over it. Seeded threads load
through GET state; the approval states run stubAssistant's draft → approve flow; streaming uses a fetch
override whose SSE body never closes. Keyboard and ARIA observations go to `keyboard-<width>.json` in the same folder.

States: closed (toggle focused, tooltip), empty-thread, thread-list, streaming-tools (one tool running),
streaming-text (deltas, no finish), answer-queue (Markdown table + list + tool chips), answer-review-page (`/items/1`),
approval-pending, approval-pending-review-page, approval-edit, approval-edit-422, approval-reject, sent, rejected
(you declined), refused (allowlist blocked), error-409, error-failed (`failed` lifecycle), outbox-empty,
outbox-loading, outbox-error, outbox-list, outbox-selected (HTML preview), outbox-selected-text (no HTML part),
outbox-panel, outbox-panel-selected; focus-approve, focus-edit.

Verdict: **2 blockers, 9 should-fix, 11 nits.** The approval gate works: the card says "Nothing is sent until you
approve", the composer locks with the reason, a 422 lands on To, and every send so far needed a click. The
blockers are both layout. The panel takes a fixed 400px grid column (`.app-assistant { w-100 }`,
`.app-shell grid-cols-[auto_minmax(0,1fr)_auto]`), and nothing beside it adapts. That leaves the page 824px at 1440
and 664px at 1280. The review screen's decision bar runs under the panel, and the Outbox's Subject column
collapses to one character. The panel's open state persists across navigation and reloads
(`clearpath.assistant.open`), so a reviewer who opens it once keeps getting these layouts.

## Keep

- No sparkle or wand: the toggle is `ChatTeardropText`. No emoji, no gradients, no shadowed cards: the panel is a
  flat column on the nav's surface (§5.6). `icons.test.ts` passes (4/4) with the five new keys (`nav.assistant`,
  `action.new`, `action.stop`, `feedback.running`, `feedback.awaiting`), so they're distinct glyphs.
  design-system.md's registry table doesn't list them yet.
- The gate is stated where the decision happens: "Email draft — needs your approval" / "Nothing is sent until you
  approve." The composer locks with "Approve, edit or reject the email first" in the placeholder and in
  `aria-describedby`, so there's never a second run racing a pending send.
- The 422 on To lands under the field with the server's own words ("Not an internal address: …"), and the rest
  of the draft is kept (`approval-edit-422-*`).
- Reject asks for an optional reason and focuses it (`approval-reject-*`). The focus ring on Approve is clear
  (`focus-approve-1440.png`).
- §5.4 is enforced where the app controls it. The system prompt bans "approved, compliant, non-compliant or a
  violation" and asks for "a suggestion with the citation" (`backend/assistant/prompt.py:48-49`). The answer text in
  these shots is fixture prose, so it isn't evidence either way. The UI's own copy (empty thread: "It can't decide
  items."; chip labels; card) never claims a decision. Markdown tables and lists render densely, and CP keys link
  inline (`answer-queue-*`).
- The Outbox renders HTML only in `sandbox=""` with `referrerPolicy="no-referrer"`. Requested by and Approved by are
  separate columns and facts (`outbox-list-*`: Sam requested, Priya approved). The plain-text fallback works
  (`outbox-selected-text-*`).
- The toggle tooltip shows the shortcut. The panel is a `complementary` region, the conversation is a `log`, and the
  card is a `region` named by its heading.

## Fixture notes (not findings)

- Ages in the thread menu and the Outbox's Sent column are relative to the wall clock, not the fixture `NOW`. "1h"
  next to a thread seeded 2 minutes before `NOW` is the stub, not RelativeAge.
- Shots on `stubApi.ts` (`approval-*`, `sent`, review page) show stat skeletons in the queue header because that stub
  has no `/items/stats`. "Context: CP-1" next to CP-1042 is the same fixture: real keys are `CP-{pk}`
  (`backend/reviews/models.py:194`).
- The make-interfaces-feel-better skill isn't installed in this environment. I used its checklist from a local copy.
  Its 40px hit-area rule is waived here as in design-system.md (28px header icon buttons, §5.3 density). The
  concentric-radius rule is waived under the 6px radius ceiling: the card is `rounded-md` 4px with 12px padding,
  the body `rounded-sm` 3px. Transitions name their properties (`.app-assistant-ask`: background-color). The
  Outbox's Sent column uses tabular-nums. Raw `<button>`s (example asks, Outbox subject) still get the global
  `:focus-visible` ring.
